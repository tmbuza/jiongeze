Published

May 2026

  • ID: JIO-P02
  • Type: Applied Project
  • Audience: Learners, Community Members
  • Theme: Organization, Communication

Supporting Information Sharing

Brochures help people learn about important topics.

Unlike flyers that usually share one short message, brochures often provide more detailed information.

Brochures may include:

  • Health information
  • Education opportunities
  • Community programs
  • Business information
  • Learning activities

Helping organize and support brochures is an important community activity.

Note

Clear information helps people understand and make better decisions.

Project Goal

Support the preparation, organization, and sharing of useful information materials.

Skills Developed

Through this project you may practice:

✓ Communication

✓ Organization

✓ Attention to detail

✓ Teamwork

✓ Responsibility

Understanding Information

Before sharing information, ask:

  • What is this brochure about?
  • Who is the audience?
  • Why is it useful?
  • What action should people take?

Understanding information helps prevent confusion.

Activity

Imagine you receive a brochure about:

Healthy Living in the Community

Main Topic

Practice Activity

Work individually or with a group:

  1. Read a brochure.

  2. Identify the main topic.

  3. Explain the information in your own words.

  4. Discuss who could benefit.

Reflection

Think about these questions:

  1. Why is organized information important?

  2. What did I learn from this activity?

  3. How can information help communities?

“Good information creates understanding.”
